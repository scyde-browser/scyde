# SCYDE v0.2.1: fixes from the first phone tests

Fixes for what the owner found on iOS and Android with v0.2.0. Install as in [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md). On Android, uninstall v0.2.0 first: it is signed with a different test key.

## Fixed
- **Pages that would not scroll.** When Shield hid a site's overlay (an "ad blocker detected" wall or a consent dialog), the site's scroll lock stayed behind. A new scroll guard unlocks scrolling whenever no visible dialog covers the page. It runs on all platforms.
- **iOS: the address bar showed the old site** (for example google.com after you opened a search result), and the back button and progress bar did not update. The island now follows the active tab.
- **Google AMP pages showed google.com.** Links like `google.com/amp/s/<site>` now open the publisher's page directly, so the address bar shows the real domain (De-AMP, also on Windows).
- **Settings, Library and Shield opened as tabs with no way to close them.** They now open as a sheet over your tabs:
  - iOS: Done, or swipe down
  - Android: ×, or back
  - A link to a website inside a sheet opens in your current tab.
- **Swiping on the island did nothing** when the swipe started on a button (most of the island). Now:
  - swipe left: next tab; after the last tab, a new one
  - swipe right: previous tab
  - swipe up: all tabs
  - The island follows your finger.
- **Android: no back/forward swipe.** Swipe in from the left edge to go back and from the right edge to go forward; an arrow shows when releasing will navigate. On iOS this was already WebKit's own edge swipe.

## Verification
- **Scroll guard:** Playwright tests in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit (`web/e2e/tests/scroll-guard.spec.mjs`), plus the new `qa/scroll` fixture.
- **Android emulator:** opens `qa/scroll` and swipes; the page must move, otherwise CI fails. The emulator also swipes the island and opens Settings as a sheet, with screenshots.
- **iOS simulator:** screenshots of `qa/scroll` (it must say "SCROLL OK") and of the Settings sheet.
- **De-AMP:** unit tests in `core/omnibox`.
