# SCYDE v0.3.0: the island grows up

The island on phones is now the whole browser: every action is one tap away, and when you read, it gets out of the way. Install as in [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md). On Android, uninstall the previous version first: each test build is signed with its own key.

## New on iOS and Android
- **The island:** the SCYDE mark (your menu) · back · address · Shield · tabs.
  - **Swipe left/right:** switch tabs; past the last tab, a new one opens.
  - **Swipe up:** all tabs.
  - **Swipe down:** full screen.
  - **Long press on the tab counter:** new tab, private tab, close tab.
- **Full screen while reading:** scrolling down hides the island and the status bar, and the page runs edge to edge. Scroll up, reach the end of the page (iOS), or tap or swipe up at the bottom edge to bring it back.
- **The menu** (tap the mark):
  - back, forward, reload, share
  - new tab, private tab
  - bookmark (star)
  - find in page
  - desktop site
  - copy link
  - history, bookmarks, settings
- **Address = command bar:** typing shows open tabs to switch to, your history and bookmarks, the search row and actions ("private", "find", "settings"…). With an empty field it shows your recent pages.
- **Shield per site:** tap the Shield light for a switch that turns Shield off for just this site and reloads it. Android also shows how many requests were blocked on the page; WebKit on iOS does not report this.
- **Private tabs:** no history, cookies or cache are kept after closing them. They have their own section in the tab grid, and Shield runs there too.
- **Peek:** long-press a link for a live preview. From there: open, open in a new tab or in the background, open privately, copy, share.
- **Aura tint:** the island takes a little of the site's own colour, only when the text stays readable (AA).
- **Welcome:** on first launch, three short steps (theme and accent, search engine, what Shield does). Each step can be skipped.
- **Settings:** a new "Tabs" section (sleep timer; sleeping tabs arrive in the next version).

## Also
- **Windows:** the welcome page on first launch.
- **De-AMP and the scroll guard** from v0.2.1 apply everywhere.

- **iOS simulator:** screenshots of New Tab, the qa fixtures, the Settings sheet and the welcome sheet.
- **Windows:** installs the app and runs the smoke test (`qa/adblock` 11/11).
