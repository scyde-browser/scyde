# SCYDE v0.4.0: tabs that remember, Spaces, and a floating island on Windows

Install as in [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md). On Android, uninstall the previous version first: each test build is signed with its own key.

## Windows
- **The island floats over the page.** The page now fills the whole window, and the island floats over it in its own transparent window. The black band behind it is gone.
- **Welcome on first launch:** theme, search engine, what Shield does.

## iOS and Android
- **Your tabs come back.** After closing the app, SCYDE reopens the tabs you had, in their Spaces. Only the tab you look at loads; the others wait until you open them.
  - Set "On startup" to "New Tab" in Settings to start fresh; pinned tabs still come back.
  - Private tabs are never saved.
- **Sleeping tabs:** a tab you have not opened for 15 minutes falls asleep and frees its memory. Open it and it wakes with its history (back and forward still work).
  - Change the time, or turn it off, in Settings → Tabs.
  - In the tab grid, sleeping tabs are dimmed.
- **Pinned tabs:** long-press a tab in the grid → Pin. Pinned tabs come first and survive "Close all".
- **Spaces:** groups of tabs, for example Personal, Work or a trip, each with its own colour.
  - Switch Spaces at the top of the tab grid.
  - Create one with "+".
  - Long-press a Space to rename it, change its colour or delete it.
  - Long-press a tab to move it to another Space.
  - With more than one Space, the island's edge shows the current Space's colour.
  - Swiping on the island and "Close all" stay inside the current Space.

## Verification
- **CI:** builds every platform. Windows installs the app and runs the smoke test (`qa/adblock` 11/11). iOS takes simulator screenshots. The Android emulator runs a scroll, island swipe and settings sheet check.
- **Session file:** local only, like history, and never synced.
