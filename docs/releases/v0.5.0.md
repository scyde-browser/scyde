# SCYDE v0.5.0: fixes and polish

No new features: this release fixes bugs and layout problems found by rendering every internal page at phone and desktop sizes and by two code reviews of the iOS and Android apps. Install as in [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md). On Android, uninstall the previous version first.

## All platforms: internal pages
- **Settings:** "Put unused tabs to sleep after" showed 1,441 buttons. It is now six choices: Off, 5, 15, 30, 60, 120 minutes.
- **New Tab:** "Where to today?" was cut off on narrow phones.
- **History:** the "Clear history" button no longer breaks into two lines.
- **Shield:** the three numbers stay on one row on phones; site rows have room.
- **Sheets on phones:** Settings, History and Shield no longer show their title twice.

## Windows
- **The island hides while you read.** Scroll down and it moves away. It returns when you scroll up, reach the top or end of the page, move the mouse to the bottom edge, or press Ctrl+L.
- **Island menu:** "Close tab" added; "Bookmarks" opens bookmarks (it opened history).
- **Shield light:** no longer shows a misleading "0" (only blocked popups can be counted on Windows).
- **Alt+F4** on the island closes the browser, not just the island.

## iOS
- **Status bar:** page text no longer shows through under the clock; the bar takes the page's colour when the clock stays readable.
- **End of page:** the last lines scroll clear of the island.
- **Shield:** the first page after launch waits for the block lists, so it is not loaded unblocked. Opening a link in a new window no longer changes Shield for the page you were on.
- **Sleeping tabs:** a tab you just left no longer falls asleep within a minute.
- **History:** reloads, back/forward and waking a tab are not counted as visits. A page without a title no longer keeps the previous page's title.
- **Address bar:** tab suggestions stay inside normal or private mode.

## Android
- **Session:** opening SCYDE from a link in another app no longer wipes your saved tabs and Spaces.
- **Island gestures:** swipe down for full screen now acts on the current tab; dragging in the address field moves the cursor instead of switching tabs.
- **Shield per site:** works for international domain names; survives the app being recreated (font size or language change).
- **Sleeping tabs:** same fix as iOS; desktop mode survives sleep.
- **Full screen:** hides after a normal scroll distance on high-density screens; in-page links no longer bring the island back.
- **History:** reloads and wakes are not counted as visits.

## Known, not fixed yet
- **Android:** the blocked count is per site, not per tab; turning Shield off does not lift GeckoView's own tracking protection; the end of a page can sit under the island.
- **Windows:** on the very first launch, block lists can take a moment to apply.

## Verification
- **Web:** Playwright in three engines, with screenshots at 390 and 1440 px in light and dark.
- **CI:** builds every platform; Windows installs and runs the smoke test; iOS simulator screenshots; Android emulator scroll, swipe and sheet check.
- **Not verified:** nothing was run on a real phone, and the native screens were reviewed by reading code, not by looking at them.
