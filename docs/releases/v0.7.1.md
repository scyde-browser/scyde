# SCYDE v0.7.1: tabs at the top on Windows

Install as in [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md).

## Windows
- **Tabs at the top.** Every open tab is shown across the top of the window with its icon and title. Click a tab to switch to it, × or a middle click closes it, + opens a new one. Before, tabs were small overlapping circles in the island with no titles.
- **A cleaner window.** The Windows title bar is gone. Minimize, maximize and close sit at the right end of the tab strip, and the empty part of the strip moves the window (double click maximizes).
- **The island** keeps back, forward, reload, the address and Shield.

## Not changed
- iOS and Android are the same as in [v0.7.0](v0.7.0.md).

## Verification
- CI builds Windows, starts the app twice and checks the ad-block test page (11/11) and the screenshot with the strip.
- **Not verified:** nothing was run on a real Windows PC. Resizing from the window edges and dragging by the strip cannot be tested in CI.
