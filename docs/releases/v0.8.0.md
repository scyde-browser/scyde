# SCYDE v0.8.0: Windows with a sidebar, Spaces and split view

Install as in [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md).

## Windows
- **Fixed: no navigation bar.** The bar at the bottom was a separate window that did not show on some PCs. It is gone. Everything is now in a sidebar inside the window, so it cannot go missing.
- **Sidebar:** back, forward, reload, Shield, the address, and all your tabs down the side with their titles.
- **Pinned tabs:** pin a tab and it becomes a tile at the top, in every Space. Right click or middle click a tile to unpin it.
- **Spaces:** groups of tabs with their own colour (work, personal…). Switch with the dots at the bottom, + adds one, double click the name to rename it.
- **Split view:** "open beside" on a tab shows two pages side by side.
- **Command bar:** Ctrl+L or Ctrl+K, then type: an address, a search, the name of an open tab, a page from history, or a command (New tab, Settings, History, Pin tab…).
- **Hide the sidebar:** Ctrl+S (or the button) gives the page the whole window. Move the mouse to the left edge to bring it back.
- **Your tabs come back** after closing the browser: tabs, pinned tabs and Spaces are restored, and each tab loads when you open it.
- **Shortcuts:** Ctrl+T new tab, Ctrl+W close, Ctrl+Shift+T reopen, Ctrl+Tab next, Ctrl+1…9 that tab.

## iOS and Android
- **The top of the screen** (behind the clock) is now always the colour of the navigation bar. It is no longer see-through, and it stays in place while you scroll. The bottom is unchanged.

## All platforms
- **New app icon colour:** the SCYDE mark is now the green of iOS call buttons.
