# SCYDE v0.9.0: Reader, favourites, tab search and tidy tabs

Install as in [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md). On Android, uninstall the previous version first.

## All platforms
- **New Tab shows your most visited sites** as tiles under the question, from your own history only.
- **Close old tabs automatically** (Settings › Tabs, off by default): tabs you have not opened for 1, 7, 30 or 90 days close on their own. Pinned tabs stay, and the pages are still in History. On Windows the setting is shown but not applied yet.

## iOS and Android
- **Reader:** Menu › Reader shows the article as clean text, without the page around it. Tap × or Reader again to go back.
- **Text size:** Menu › Smaller text / Larger text, 50–200 %.
- **Search tabs:** the tab grid has a search field that looks through all your tabs, in every Space.

## iOS
- **Pull down to reload** at the top of a page.
- **Fixed: the top line of New Tab was hidden** behind the bar at the top of the screen in v0.8.0.

## Verification
- CI builds all three platforms and publishes screenshots.
- Reader was tested in a desktop browser on a test article.
- **Not verified:** nothing was run on a real phone. Reader, text size, tab search, pull to reload and closing old tabs are not covered by the CI smoke tests.
